Find and add members

Search the member list, add a new member by hand, and delete members you no longer need.

{{i:map-pin}} Where{{i:users}} Members
{{h:steps|Find a member}}
  1. Type in the search box {{n:1}}

    Part of a name or email is enough. Upper and lower case do not matter. The list updates a moment after you stop typing.

  2. Open the member {{n:6}}

    Select their name or email, or the arrow at the end of the row. See A member's page.

The list shows 10 members per page, newest first. Use Next and Prev {{n:7}} to page through.

{{h:steps|Add a member}}
  1. Select New member

    At the top right of the Members page.

  2. Enter the Name
  3. Enter the Email

    It must be a valid address that no other member uses.

  4. Tick any Segments

    Optional. Joining a segment also hands out the segment's challenges that are still active and not past their deadline.

  5. Fill in custom fields

    Optional. They start with each field's default value.

  6. Select Create member"Member created." The list reloads. The new member is not opened.
{{h:opts|Member fields}}
{{o:Name|req}}The member's full name.{{/o}} {{o:Email|req}}Must be unique. The member app sends sign-in codes here.{{/o}} {{o:Phone|opt}}Any text. Not checked.{{/o}} {{o:Segments|opt}}The segments the member is in. See Segments.{{/o}} {{o:Custom attributes|opt}}Fields your program added, such as a favourite store. See Custom member fields.{{/o}}
{{h:rules|Good to know}}
  • A new member is added to every program, with 0 points in each.
  • The member gets a tier straight away if a tier matches. A tier with no conditions matches everyone.
  • Search looks at name and email only, not phone. There is no filter by tier or segment.
  • The line under the Members title counts all members in the program, even while you search.
  • Email addresses must be unique, but only the part after the @ ignores case. "Ada@example.com" and "ada@example.com" can be two members.
{{h:steps|Delete several members}}
  1. Tick the members {{n:4}}

    The box in the header ticks every member on this page. Ticks stay while you page and search.

  2. Select Delete selected {{n:5}}
  3. Confirm with Delete 2 members"2 members deleted." The ticks are cleared.
{{h:risk|Deleting a member}}

Deleting removes the person from every program, with all their points, redemptions, purchases, challenges, segments and events. It cannot be undone. Ticked members that your current search hides are deleted too, so check the count in the window. If one of them was already deleted, nobody is deleted and you see "Member(s) not found: ...".

{{h:fix|Messages}}
What you seeWhat to do
"A member with this email already exists"Search for that email. The person is already a member.
"value is not a valid email address: ..."Fix the email address.
"No segments yet" in the Segments boxCreate a segment first on the Segments page.

A member's page

See everything about one member in the selected program, and edit their profile, tier inputs and segments.

{{i:map-pin}} Where{{i:users}} Members›a member
{{h:info|What is on the page}}

The profile card at the top shows the name, email, tier and segments {{n:1}}, then the phone, email verification and custom fields. Under it are five totals {{n:7}} and these cards:

{{o:Points activity}}Every points change: Earn, Spend or Adjust, with the amount, the reason and the time. The newest 200 are shown.{{/o}} {{o:Challenges}}The member's challenges with progress. See A member's challenges.{{/o}} {{o:Redemptions & prizes}}Rewards the member redeemed for points ("Redeemed") or was given ("Prize"), and whether prizes were claimed.{{/o}} {{o:Purchases}}What the member bought in the member app, with quantity and total.{{/o}} {{o:Events}}Events your systems sent for this member and what their rules did. Select a row to see the full event. See Test an event for Send event.{{/o}}
{{h:steps|Edit the profile}}
  1. Select Edit {{n:3}}

    The card turns into a form in place.

  2. Change what you need

    Name and email are the two boxes at the top. Below are Phone, the Verified tick, custom fields and Segments.

  3. Select Save changes"Member updated."
[[warn Wait for segments to load]]

Save only after the Segments list has loaded. Saving while it still says "Loading segments…" removes the member from all their segments.

[[/]] {{h:rules|Good to know}}
  • Name, email and phone belong to the person, so a change shows in every program.
  • The tier updates on its own from points balance, purchases, segments and custom attributes. You cannot pick a tier by hand.
  • You cannot clear a phone number once it is set. Replace it instead.
  • The Verified tick is normally set by the verification email. Every save with it ticked sets the verification date to today. Saving with it unticked removes the verification, in every program.
  • Saving the profile also hands out again any active challenge of the member's segments that they do not have, including ones you removed.
  • While you edit, the Points and Redeem / assign buttons are greyed out.
  • The lists on this page show the newest 200 entries. Lifetime earned adds up only Earn entries among the newest 200 points changes, so positive adjustments are not counted.
  • Copy ID {{n:2}} copies the member's ID in this program. The same person has a different ID in each program.
{{h:risk|Delete a member}}

Delete {{n:4}} removes the person from every program, with all their points, redemptions and challenge history. Confirm with Delete member. "Member deleted." and you are back on the Members list. It cannot be undone.

{{h:fix|Messages}}
What you seeWhat to do
"Member not found"The member was deleted, or the link belongs to another program. Switch program, or search for the person again.
"Email already registered"Another person uses that email.

Earn, burn and adjust points

Add points to a member, take points away, or correct their balance by hand, with a reason that shows in their history.

{{i:map-pin}} Where{{i:users}} Members›a member›Points:
  1. Open the member and select Earn

    In the Points: row at the bottom of the profile card.

  2. Enter the Points

    A whole number above 0, before the tier multiplier.

  3. Enter a Reason

    Optional. It shows in Points activity.

  4. Select Add points"Points earned." Check Points activity for the amount actually added. Burn says "Points burned." and Adjust says "Balance adjusted."
{{h:opts|The three actions}}
ButtonWindowAmountShows in history as
{{i:arrow-up}} EarnEarn points: Add pointsAbove 0. The member's tier multiplier is applied.Earn, in green
{{i:arrow-down}} BurnBurn points: Burn pointsAbove 0. No more than the balance.Spend, in red
{{i:sparkles}} AdjustAdjust balance: Apply adjustmentAny whole number except 0. Use a negative value to subtract.Adjust
{{h:ex|Example: the multiplier}}

Maya is Gold, with an earn rate of 1.5×. You earn 100 points for her. She gets 150. A Burn or Adjust of 100 changes her balance by exactly 100.

{{h:rules|Good to know}}
  • A balance can never go below 0.
  • The tier is checked again after every points change, so a member can move up or down a tier.
  • Tiers look at the current balance. Burning points can drop a member to a lower tier.
  • Points after the multiplier are rounded to a whole number. Halves round to the nearest even number. With a multiplier below 0.5, earning 1 point records 0.
  • Points are per program.
{{h:fix|Messages}}
What you seeWhat to do
"Enter a positive number of points."Enter a whole number above 0.
"Insufficient points: has 40, needs 100"Burn no more than the balance.
"Adjustment would result in negative balance"Use a smaller negative amount.
"Enter a non-zero adjustment (+ or -)."Enter a number other than 0.

Rewards and prizes for a member

Redeem a reward with a member's points, or give it to them as a free prize, with an optional email to claim it.

{{i:map-pin}} Where{{i:users}} Members›a member›Redeem / assign
  1. Open the member and select Redeem / assign
  2. Choose whether to email free prizes

    Optional. Tick it before you select Assign free.

  3. Select Assign free to give it away"Prize assigned." or "Prize assigned and emailed."
  4. Or select Redeem to pay with points"Reward redeemed." The points cost leaves the balance.

Redeem is greyed out {{n:4}} when the member does not have enough points. The window stays open, so you can give several rewards in a row.

{{h:rules|Good to know}}
  • Only active rewards that are in stock are listed.
  • Neither button asks to confirm. Each click gives the reward again, so select once and wait for the message.
  • A claim email link works for 30 days. Only the newest link for a prize works.
  • If the email fails, the prize is still assigned.
  • Redeeming lowers the balance, which can lower the member's tier.

A member's challenges

Give one member a challenge, record their progress, complete it for them, or take it away.

{{i:map-pin}} Where{{i:users}} Members›a member›Challenges
  1. Select Assign challenge

    Pick a challenge and select Assign."Challenge assigned."

  2. Select Progress to record progress

    Enter an Amount (default 1) and select Add progress."Progress recorded."

  3. Select Complete to finish it now"Challenge completed." Points and prize are paid at once.
  4. Select the bin to remove it

    Confirm with Remove."Challenge removed."

{{h:rules|Good to know}}
  • Reaching the target completes the challenge on its own and pays out.
  • The payout is the challenge's reward points, plus its prize reward if that reward is active and in stock. Challenge points do not get the tier multiplier.
  • Complete pays out even if progress is short or the deadline has passed. It does not ask to confirm.
  • Removing a challenge keeps any points already paid. You can assign it again, and it starts from 0.
  • A member can have each challenge only once at a time.
  • A challenge past its deadline keeps its old status until progress is added, by you or by an event rule. Then it shows Expired.
  • Progress shows only for Assigned and In progress challenges. Complete also shows for Expired ones.
  • The Note in the Add progress window is not saved.
{{h:fix|Messages}}
What you seeWhat to do
"Nothing to assign"The member has every active challenge, or none are active.
"Challenge has expired"When assigning: the challenge's end date has passed, so edit the challenge. When adding progress: this member's own deadline has passed, and editing the challenge does not change it. Remove and assign it again, or select Complete.
"Challenge already assigned to this member"Remove the old one first if you want them to start again.
"Challenge is already completed"Nothing to do. It has paid out.

Custom member fields

Add your own fields to every member, such as a birthday or favourite store, so you can store them, use them in tiers and set them from events.

{{i:map-pin}} Where{{i:users}} Members›{{i:sliders-horizontal}} Configure
{{h:steps|Add a field}}
  1. Select New custom attribute
  2. Enter a Name

    The first letter or digit must be a letter. The internal key under it is made from the name's letters and digits.

  3. Choose the Type

    Text, Number, Yes / No, Date or Dropdown. It cannot change later.

  4. For a dropdown, add the Options

    Press Enter or a comma after each one.

  5. Choose a Default value

    Optional. It is written onto every existing member straight away.

  6. Select Create attribute"Custom attribute created."
{{h:opts|Field settings}}
{{o:Name|req;lim=1 to 100 characters}}The first letter or digit must be a letter (A to Z). Spaces, punctuation and accented letters are left out of the key. You can rename it later; the key stays.{{/o}} {{o:Type|req;def=Text}}
TextNumberYes / NoDateDropdown
Fixed once created.{{/o}} {{o:Options|lim=At least 1}}Dropdown only. Duplicates are dropped.{{/o}} {{o:Default value|opt}}On create: applied to every existing member. On edit: only for members created from now on.{{/o}}
{{h:rules|Good to know}}
  • Custom fields are always optional. A member can leave any of them empty.
  • Text values can be up to 500 characters.
  • The internal key {{n:2}} is what your systems and event rules use. It is camelCase, such as favouriteStore, and never changes.
  • Each program has its own custom fields.
  • A Yes / No field always gets the default "No", even if you leave the default unticked.
  • Removing a dropdown option keeps old values on members, but the next time someone edits that member, the value is cleared.

The Built-in fields card above lists the fields every member has: Name, Email, Phone, Tier, Points balance and Segments. They cannot be changed.

{{h:risk|Delete a field}}

Delete {{n:4}} removes the field and its value from every member in this program. It cannot be undone. Conditions on the field then see it as empty: "is" stops matching, but "is not" matches everyone. Members keep their tier until their next points, purchase or profile change.
