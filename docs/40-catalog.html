Segments

A segment is a named group of members, such as VIP or Newsletter. Use segments to hand out challenges to a group and as tier conditions.

{{i:map-pin}} Where{{i:tag}} Segments
{{h:steps|Create a segment}}
  1. Select New segment {{n:1}}
  2. Enter a Name

    It must be unique in this program.

  3. Add a Description and Color

    Both optional. The colour tints the segment's icon.

  4. Select Create segment"Segment created."
{{h:steps|Add members to a segment}}
  1. Select Assign members on the segment's card
  2. Search by name or email

    Members already in the segment are not listed.

  3. Tick Select all, or
  4. Tick single members

    Ticks stay while you change the search.

  5. Select Assign"Members assigned." The window closes.

You can also set a member's segments in New member, on their page, or with an event rule.

{{h:opts|Segment settings}}
{{o:Name|req}}Unique in the program. "VIP" and "vip" count as different names.{{/o}} {{o:Description|opt}}Shown on the card.{{/o}} {{o:Color|opt}}A hex value such as #f59e0b, or pick one with the swatch. Other text is saved as is, but does not tint the icon.{{/o}}
{{h:rules|Good to know}}
  • Joining a segment hands out every active challenge that was assigned to that segment, unless it has expired.
  • Members who join get their tier checked again.
  • Segments are listed in alphabetical order.
  • Assign members lists the newest 1,000 members only.
  • Once set, a description or colour cannot be cleared, only replaced.
{{h:risk|Delete a segment}}

Delete (the bin {{n:3}}) takes the segment off every member and off every challenge it was assigned to. Challenges members already got stay. Tiers that use the segment stop matching, but members keep their current tier until something else changes it. It cannot be undone.

{{h:fix|Messages}}
What you seeWhat to do
"Segment name already exists"Pick another name.
"Everyone's already in"Every one of the newest 1,000 members is in the segment already, or the program has no members yet.

Rewards

Rewards are what members spend points on, or win as prizes. Set the points cost, limit the stock, and turn rewards on and off.

{{i:map-pin}} Where{{i:gift}} Rewards
{{h:steps|Create a reward}}
  1. Select New reward
  2. Enter a Name

    For example "$10 gift card".

  3. Set the Points cost
  4. Set the Stock, or leave it blank

    Blank means unlimited.

  5. Keep Active ticked to offer it now
  6. Select Create reward"Reward created."
{{h:opts|Reward settings}}
{{o:Name|req}}Shown to staff, in prize emails and in the member's Wallet.{{/o}} {{o:Description|opt}}Details shown to members.{{/o}} {{o:Points cost|req;lim=Whole number above 0}}What a redemption takes from the balance.{{/o}} {{o:Stock|opt;def=Unlimited;lim=0 or more}}How many are left. Every redemption and every free prize uses one.{{/o}} {{o:Active (available to redeem)|def=On}}Only active rewards can be redeemed or given.{{/o}}
{{h:steps|Turn a reward off, copy, edit or delete it}}
  1. Open the actions menu {{n:2}}

    The {{i:ellipsis-vertical}} button at the end of the row.

  2. Pick an action {{n:3}}

    Deactivate or Activate work at once. Copy ID copies the reward's ID. Edit and Delete are meant to open a window.

[[warn Known issue: Edit and Delete in the menu]]

In the current version, selecting Edit or Delete closes the menu without opening the window. The same happens on Products. Until it is fixed, Deactivate a reward you no longer want to offer.

[[/]] {{h:rules|Good to know}}
  • A reward can be handed out when it is active and its stock is blank or above 0.
  • When stock reaches 0, the Stock column says "Out of stock" {{n:4}}, but the reward still shows Active.
  • Stock never goes back up on its own. Raising it needs Edit (see the known issue below).
  • Members cannot redeem rewards themselves in the member app. Staff redeem or assign them on the member's page, and event rules and challenges can give them out.
  • Reward names do not have to be unique. The list has no fixed order.
  • Event rules that give a deleted reward stay, and that effect is skipped.
[[warn A redeemed reward cannot be deleted]]

While any member still has a redemption or prize of this reward, deleting it fails with "The request conflicts with existing data." Deactivate it instead.

[[/]] {{h:fix|Messages}}
What you seeWhat to do
The browser's own message, such as "Value must be greater than or equal to 1"Enter a whole number of 1 or more in Points cost.
"The request conflicts with existing data."The reward has redemptions. Deactivate it instead of deleting it.

Products

Products are things members can buy in the member app. Purchases are recorded, not paid for, and the spend feeds tiers.

{{i:map-pin}} Where{{i:shopping-bag}} Products
{{h:steps|Create a product}}
  1. Select New product {{n:1}}
  2. Enter the Name and Price

    The price is in euros, with up to 2 decimals.

  3. Add a Description and Category

    Optional.

  4. Keep Active (visible to members) ticked
  5. Select Create product"Product created."
{{h:opts|Product settings}}
{{o:Name|req}}Shown in the member app catalogue.{{/o}} {{o:Description|opt}}Shown when a member opens the product.{{/o}} {{o:Price|req;lim=At least 0.01}}In the product's currency, shown next to the field. New products are always EUR, and the currency cannot be changed.{{/o}} {{o:Category|opt}}A free text group, such as "coffee".{{/o}} {{o:Active (visible to members)|def=On}}Only active products show in the member app.{{/o}}

The actions menu {{n:2}} works like the one on Rewards: Deactivate or Activate, Copy ID, Edit and Delete.

{{h:rules|Good to know}}
  • Members buy one unit at a time with Buy in the member app. No money moves and no card is charged.
  • Buying does not earn points. It counts toward lifetime purchase spend and count, which tiers can use.
  • Deleting a product keeps past purchases, with the name and price at the time.
  • Staff cannot record a purchase in the console.

Challenges

A challenge is a goal, such as five visits, that pays points, a prize or both when a member reaches it. Create challenges and hand them out to whole segments.

{{i:map-pin}} Where{{i:target}} Challenges
{{h:steps|Create a challenge}}
  1. Select New challenge and enter a Name
  2. Set the Target

    How much progress completes it, such as 5 visits.

  3. Set Reward points

    Optional. Paid when it completes.

  4. Pick a Prize reward

    Optional. Given free when it completes.

  5. Set a fixed deadline

    Optional. One date for everyone.

  6. Or set Expires after (days)

    Optional. Each member gets their own deadline, counted from when they get the challenge.

  7. Select Create challenge"Challenge created."
{{h:opts|Challenge settings}}
{{o:Name|req}}Shown on cards and member pages.{{/o}} {{o:Description|opt}}{{/o}} {{o:Target|req;def=1;lim=Whole number above 0}}Progress needed to complete.{{/o}} {{o:Reward points|opt;def=0}}Granted on completion, without the tier multiplier.{{/o}} {{o:Prize reward|opt;def=No prize}}Assigned on completion if the reward is active and in stock. Otherwise it is skipped without a message.{{/o}} {{o:Expires at|opt}}Without Expires after (days), this is everyone's deadline. With it, this is only the cut-off: after it passes, nobody new can get the challenge. Shown in your time zone.{{/o}} {{o:Expires after (days)|opt}}Each member's deadline is this many days after they get it. Leave blank to use the fixed date.{{/o}} {{o:Active (can be assigned)|def=On}}Only active challenges can be handed out. Turning it off does not stop progress for members who already have it.{{/o}}
{{h:steps|Hand out a challenge to a segment}}
  1. Select Assign to segment {{n:4}}

    On the challenge card.

  2. Choose a Segment

    Every member currently in it gets the challenge.

  3. Select Assign"Assigned to 58 member(s); 0 already had it." The segment appears under Assigned segments {{n:3}}.

To give a challenge to one member, use their page. Event rules can also assign challenges and add progress.

{{h:rules|Good to know}}
  • After you assign a challenge to a segment, people who join that segment later get it too, while it is active and not expired.
  • Assigning to an empty segment records nothing, so later joiners do not get it.
  • Challenge prizes are not emailed. They appear in the member's Wallet to claim.
  • The Prize reward list also shows inactive rewards. Those are skipped on completion.
  • Members who already have the challenge, in any status, are skipped.
  • A member's deadline is set when they get the challenge. Editing the deadline later does not change deadlines already handed out.
  • A challenge whose fixed deadline passed still shows Active on its card, but cannot be handed out.
{{h:risk|Delete a challenge}}

Deleting removes the challenge and every member's progress on it. Points and prizes already paid stay. It cannot be undone.

{{h:fix|Messages}}
What you seeWhat to do
The browser's own message, such as "Value must be greater than or equal to 1"Enter a whole number of 1 or more in Target.
"Challenge is not active"Edit the challenge and tick Active (can be assigned).
"Challenge has expired"When handing it out: move the Expires at date into the future. When adding progress: that member's own deadline passed, and editing the challenge does not change it.
"Couldn't load challenges"Select Retry.

Tiers

Tiers are levels such as Silver and Gold. Each tier has conditions and an earn rate multiplier. Loyalty Engine puts each member in the right tier on its own. A member who matches no tier has none.

{{i:map-pin}} Where{{i:layers}} Tiers
{{h:steps|Create a tier}}
  1. Select New tier and enter a Name
  2. Set the Rank

    Higher ranks are tried first.

  3. Set the Multiplier

    1.5 means members earn 1.5 times the points, rounded to a whole number.

  4. Select Add condition

    A new tier starts with no conditions. Each click adds a row.

  5. Pick a field, a comparison and a value

    All conditions must match. Number values are whole numbers.

  6. Select Create tier"Tier created." Every member in the program is checked again.
{{h:info|How members get a tier}}

Loyalty Engine tries tiers from the highest rank down and gives the member the first tier whose conditions all match. If none match, the member has no tier. Tiers are shown lowest rank first on the page.

{{h:opts|Tier settings}}
{{o:Name|req}}Unique in the program.{{/o}} {{o:Rank|req;def=0}}Whole number. Highest tried first. Two tiers with the same rank: the older one wins.{{/o}} {{o:Multiplier|req;def=1;lim=Above 0, in steps of 0.1}}Applies to points from Earn and from event rules. Below 1 lowers earning.{{/o}} {{o:Conditions|opt}}None means every member qualifies. Useful for a catch-all lowest tier.{{/o}}

{{h:rules|When tiers are checked}}
  • After every points change, purchase and member edit.
  • After an event that matched at least one active rule.
  • For every member, when you create, edit or delete a tier.
  • Not on a schedule. Nothing changes just because time passes.
  • Not when you delete a segment or a custom field.
{{h:rules|Good to know}}
  • Points balance is the current balance, not lifetime earnings. Spending points can move a member down.
  • The multiplier does not apply to Burn, Adjust, challenge points or purchases.
  • There is no OR inside a tier. For "A or B", make two tiers.
{{h:risk|Delete a tier}}

Every member is checked again. Members of the deleted tier move to another tier, or are left without one if none match. It cannot be undone.
